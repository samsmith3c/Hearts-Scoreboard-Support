♠ Frequently asked questions

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. Your in-progress game is saved on your device automatically after every hand. Reopen the app and you'll be right back at the table.

How does game history sync between my devices?

Finished games are stored in your personal iCloud account and sync automatically to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, history is kept on the device instead.

What are share links?

After a game you can share a recap link with the other players. The whole recap is packed into the link itself — whoever opens it sees the final scores, and if they have the app installed it opens right in Hearts Scoreboard.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center sign-in is optional and only used to label your own stats. Everything else works without it.

How many players are supported?

Three to six players, with the standard Hearts scoring rules adjusted for each table size.
